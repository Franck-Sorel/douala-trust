# ASM-yaounde-douala-market: First Market Is Domestic Yaoundé ↔ Douala

**Category**: Business

**Status**: Unverified

**Risk if wrong**: High — if the first buyers are not domestic Cameroonians buying from
Douala, the MVP market, verifier logistics and transport assumptions change materially.

## Statement

The first audience is citizens of Yaoundé, Cameroon, who want to buy products located in
Douala, Cameroon. Buyers and verifiers are both in-country; the buyer's problem is
distance/absence, not international reach (corrected from an earlier assumption that buyers
were abroad).

## Rationale

This is the domestic trust gap: a Yaoundé buyer cannot inspect a product in a Douala market.
An independent Douala verifier produces evidence the buyer reviews remotely. Buyers are
physically reachable in-country, which simplifies custody/transport (can be deferred or
handled locally).

## Verification Plan

Confirm with the first 3–5 pilot verification transactions that real Yaoundé buyers want,
use, and will pay for Douala verification evidence. This is the MVP's core experiment.
