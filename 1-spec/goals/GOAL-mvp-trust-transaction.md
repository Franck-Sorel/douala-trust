# GOAL-mvp-trust-transaction: Prove a Remote Buyer Can Act on Verifier Evidence

**Description**: Prove the core trust hypothesis with ONE complete real-world verification
transaction: a Yaoundé buyer defines requirements, a Douala verifier inspects the physical
product and captures an evidence package, and the buyer reviews it and makes a decision —
establishing whether buyers actually trust and use the service.

**Status**: Draft

**Priority**: Must-have

**Source stakeholder**: [STK-platform](../stakeholders.md) and [STK-buyer](../stakeholders.md)

## Success Criteria

- [ ] A real Yaoundé buyer can create a verification request with concrete requirements for
      a real product located in Douala.
- [ ] A Douala verifier can inspect the product, confirm its identity, and capture evidence
      for each requirement (PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE / NOT_TESTED).
- [ ] The buyer can review the scoped evidence package and record a decision
      (ACCEPT / REJECT / REQUEST_MORE_EVIDENCE).
- [ ] The platform runs at least 3 pilot transactions and captures buyer willingness to use
      and pay for the service.
